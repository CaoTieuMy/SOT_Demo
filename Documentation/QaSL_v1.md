## Project Insights
> The project establishes a strong foundation for future data management by reducing bottlenecks and silos while enabling scalability without compromising data governance. It builds an efficient data products infrastructure that enables users to share, discover, and use data across domains, maximizing data-driven value at scale. The implemented data-mesh solution supports better decision-making, cost savings, and revenue growth for VW while tracking compliance with project specifications and milestones.



## Business Needs

#### 1. Enable data as a product

Data was not treated as a product, preventing cross-domain utilization, value creation, and data-driven decision-making at scale.

#### 2. Eliminate fragmented data processes

VW faced isolated data processes without cross-domain availability, causing inefficiencies and limited data accessibility across the organization.

#### 3. Establish scalable data management

VW lacked a solid foundation for scalable data management, making it difficult to evolve its data infrastructure without compromising governance.



## Provided Services
### Cloud/ onPrem: OnPrem

#### 1. Agile project planning and steering

Reply supports planning and steering of the project and development team through agile principles, dedicated roles, and ceremonies to enable effective collaboration, prompt issue resolution, and continuous improvement.

#### 2. Agile IT-PEP process enablement

Reply experts facilitate adherence to agile IT-PEP procedures, documentation, and processes, promoting transparent stakeholder communication and timely milestone achievement.

#### 3. Data lake analytics platform implementation

The platform connects diverse data sources without data movement and provides fast, accurate access across on-premise, cloud, and hybrid cloud environments through federated queries and fine-grained access controls.

#### 4. Data-mesh solution implementation

Through interactive workshops, Reply evaluated the existing infrastructure and implemented a robust data-mesh solution, including federated computational governance and a self-service data platform.

## Mastering Challenges

#### 1. Authorization and monitoring infrastructure integration

Integrating the data-mesh solution with VW's existing authorization and monitoring infrastructure required deep alignment with internal security and compliance standards.

#### 2. Tailored workflow implementation

VW-requested workflows required a highly tailored solution to address unique operational requirements, processes, and organizational constraints.

#### 3. Cultural shift toward data as a product

Establishing data as a product required overcoming ingrained cultural habits and resistance through continuous coaching, communication, and stakeholder engagement across domains.
