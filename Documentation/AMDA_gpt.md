## Project Insights
> Ongoing DevOps project for custom application monitoring of workloads on an OpenShift-based big-data platform. Its goal was to improve development and deployment processes for monitoring, alerting, and visualization of metrics and logs, increasing the monitoring team's efficiency. The solution was deployed for three productive Data Analytics Platform applications; additional rollouts are ongoing or planned.



## Business Needs

#### 1. Improve platform transparency and early issue detection

Establish centralized, consistent, and understandable monitoring and observability to make system behavior, stability, bottlenecks, and emerging anomalies transparent across interacting components. Enable teams to identify whether issues are infrastructure-, application-, or process-related before they become incidents.

#### 2. Reduce incident resolution time

Improve the correlation and structure of logs, metrics, and events so teams can identify root causes and responsible responders faster. Support retrospective analysis of incidents, affected components, dependencies, and recurring patterns to reduce downtime and improve cross-team coordination.

#### 3. Enable continuous operational improvement

Provide a consistent view of operational quality—including stability, availability, performance, error frequency, and recurring anomalies—for technical teams and project stakeholders. Allow risks to be identified earlier, measures to be prioritized, and technical optimizations to be justified transparently.



## Provided Services
### Cloud/ onPrem: OnPrem

#### 1. Automated deployment pipeline

Implemented a CI/CD pipeline for automated deployment of dashboards, alerts, and workloads.

#### 2. Infrastructure-as-Code workspace

Built an Infrastructure-as-Code workspace using Helm packages and Docker images.

#### 3. Custom monitoring and visualization

Developed a custom monitoring tool for Trino queries and Grafana dashboards for metric visualization, providing real-time visibility into system health and resource consumption.
