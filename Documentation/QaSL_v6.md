## Project Insights
> The project establishes a strong foundation for future data management by reducing bottlenecks and silos while enabling scalability without compromising data governance. It aims to build an efficient Data Products Infrastructure stack and promote a mindset that treats data as a product, enabling users to share, discover, and use data across domains to deliver data-driven value at scale. Reply implemented a data-mesh solution to support better decision-making, cost savings, and revenue growth for VW while tracking compliance with project specifications and milestones.



## Business Needs

#### 1. Data as a Product

Data was not treated as a product, preventing cross-domain utilization and value creation and hindering data-driven decision-making at scale.

#### 2. Fragmented Data Processes

VW faced fragmented and isolated data processes without cross-domain availability, leading to inefficiencies and limited data accessibility across the organization.

#### 3. Scalable Data Management Foundation

VW lacked a solid foundation for scalable data management, making it difficult to grow and adapt its data infrastructure to future needs without compromising governance.



## Provided Services
### Cloud/ onPrem: OnPrem

#### 1. Agile Project and Development Steering

Reply supported planning and steering of the project and development team through Agile principles, dedicated roles, and ceremonies to ensure effective collaboration, prompt issue resolution, and continuous improvement.

#### 2. Agile IT-PEP Governance and Documentation

Reply experts facilitated adherence to agile IT-PEP procedures, documentation, and processes, promoting transparent stakeholder communication and timely milestone achievement.

#### 3. Data Lake Analytics Platform

The data lake analytics platform enabled connectivity to diverse data sources without data movement, providing fast and accurate access to on-premise, cloud, or hybrid-cloud data through federated queries and fine-grained access controls.

#### 4. Data-Mesh Solution Implementation

Based on interactive workshops, Reply evaluated existing infrastructure and implemented a robust data-mesh solution, including federated computational governance and a self-service data platform to support collaborative data management.

## Mastering Challenges

#### 1. VW Authorization and Monitoring Infrastructure Integration

Integrating the data-mesh solution with VW's existing authorization and monitoring infrastructure required deep alignment with internal security and compliance standards.

#### 2. Tailored Workflow Implementation for Unique Operational Needs

Implementing VW-requested workflows required a highly tailored solution to address unique operational requirements, processes, and organizational constraints.

#### 3. Cultural Mindset Shift Towards Data as a Product

Driving the organizational change needed to treat data as a product required continuous coaching, communication, and stakeholder engagement across domains to overcome established habits and resistance.
