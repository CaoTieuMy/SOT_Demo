## Project Insights
> The project established a foundation for future data management by reducing bottlenecks and data silos while enabling scalability without compromising data governance. It aimed to build an efficient Data Products Infrastructure stack and promote a data-as-a-product mindset, enabling users to share, discover, and use data across domains at scale. Reply implemented a data-mesh solution to support better decision-making, cost savings, and revenue growth for VW while tracking compliance with project specifications and milestones.



## Business Needs

#### 1. Data as a Product

Data was not treated as a product, limiting cross-domain use, value creation, and data-driven decision-making at scale.

#### 2. Fragmented Data Processes

Isolated data processes and a lack of cross-domain availability caused inefficiency and poor data accessibility across the organization.

#### 3. Scalable Data Management Foundation

VW lacked a solid foundation for scalable data management, making it difficult to evolve its data infrastructure without compromising governance.



## Provided Services
### Cloud/ onPrem: OnPrem

#### 1. Agile Project and Development Steering

Reply supported project and development-team planning and steering through Agile principles, dedicated roles, and ceremonies to enable effective collaboration, prompt issue resolution, and continuous improvement.

#### 2. Agile IT-PEP Enablement

Reply facilitated adherence to Agile IT-PEP procedures, documentation, and processes, supporting transparent stakeholder communication and timely milestone achievement.

#### 3. Data Lake Analytics Platform

A data lake analytics platform was provided to connect diverse data sources without data movement, enabling fast and accurate access across on-premise, cloud, and hybrid environments through federated queries and fine-grained access controls.

## Mastering Challenges

#### 1. Authorization and Monitoring Infrastructure Integration

Integrating the data-mesh solution with VW's existing authorization and monitoring infrastructure required deep alignment with internal security and compliance standards.

#### 2. Tailored Workflow Implementation

VW-requested workflows required a tailored solution that addressed unique operational requirements beyond standard approaches.

#### 3. Data-as-a-Product Mindset Shift

Establishing data as a product required overcoming entrenched cultural habits and resistance through ongoing coaching, communication, and stakeholder engagement across domains.
