## Project Insights
> The project aimed to establish a strong foundation for future data management by reducing bottlenecks and silos while enabling scalability without compromising data governance. It focused on an efficient Data Products Infrastructure stack and on treating data as a product, so users could share, discover, and use data across domains to create data-driven value at scale. Reply implemented a data-mesh solution to support better decision-making, cost savings, revenue growth, and compliance with project specifications and milestones.



## Business Needs

#### 1. Data as a Product

Data was not treated as a product, preventing cross-domain utilization and value creation and hindering data-driven decision-making at scale.

#### 2. Fragmented Data Processes

VW faced fragmented, isolated data processes without cross-domain availability, leading to inefficiencies and limited data accessibility across the organization.

#### 3. Scalable Data Management Foundation

VW lacked a solid foundation for scalable data management, making it difficult to grow and adapt the data infrastructure to future needs without compromising governance.



## Provided Services
### Cloud/ onPrem: OnPrem

#### 1. Agile Project and Team Steering

Reply supported the planning and steering of the project and development team through Agile principles, dedicated roles, and ceremonies to ensure effective collaboration, prompt issue resolution, and continuous improvement.

#### 2. Agile IT-PEP Process Enablement

Reply experts facilitated adherence to Agile IT-PEP procedures, documentation, and processes, promoting transparent stakeholder communication and timely milestone achievement.

#### 3. Data Lake Analytics Platform

A data lake analytics platform enabled connectivity to diverse data sources without data movement, providing fast and accurate access across on-premise, cloud, and hybrid-cloud data through federated queries and fine-grained access controls.

## Mastering Challenges

#### 1. VW Authorization and Monitoring Infrastructure Integration

Integrating the data-mesh solution with VW's existing authorization and monitoring infrastructure required deep alignment with internal security and compliance standards.

#### 2. Tailored Workflow Implementation for Unique Operational Needs

VW-requested workflows required a highly tailored solution beyond standard approaches to address specific operational processes and constraints.

#### 3. Cultural Mindset Shift Towards Data as a Product

Treating data as a product required overcoming established cultural habits and resistance through continuous coaching, communication, and stakeholder engagement across domains.
