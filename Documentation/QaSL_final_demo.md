## Project Insights
> With the need for a strong foundation for future data management, the purpose of the project is to reduce bottlenecks and silos in data management while allowing scalability – without compromising on data governance. The goal is to have an efficient Data Products Infrastructure stack, shifting mindset and treating data as a product, enabling users to share, discover and utilize data from other domains – maximizing and delivering data-driven value at scale. Using cutting-edge technologies, Reply implemented an innovative data-mesh solution, leading to better decision making, saving costs and boosting revenues for VW. Reply tracks compliance with project specifications and milestones to achieve VW's data mesh goal.

## Business Needs

#### 1. Foundation for Future Data Management

Need for a strong, scalable foundation for data management that reduces bottlenecks and silos without compromising data governance.

#### 2. Efficient Data Products Infrastructure

Requirement for an efficient Data Products Infrastructure stack that treats data as a product, enabling users to share, discover, and utilize data across domains.

#### 3. Data-Driven Value at Scale

Need to maximize and deliver data-driven value at scale by enabling cross-domain data access and utilization.

#### 1. VW Authorization and Monitoring Infrastructure Integration

Integrating the data-mesh solution with VW's existing authorization and monitoring infrastructure posed significant technical complexity, requiring deep alignment with internal security and compliance standards.

#### 2. Tailored Workflow Implementation for Unique Operational Needs

Implementing VW-requested workflows demanded a highly tailored solution to address unique operational requirements, going beyond standard approaches to fit the specific processes and constraints of the organization.

#### 3. Cultural Mindset Shift Towards Data as a Product

Driving the organizational change needed to treat data as a product required overcoming deeply rooted cultural habits and resistance, necessitating continuous coaching, communication, and stakeholder engagement across domains.
