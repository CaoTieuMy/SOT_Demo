## Project Insights
> With the need for a strong foundation for future data management, the project aims to reduce bottlenecks and silos in data management while allowing scalability — without compromising on data governance. The goal is to have an efficient Data Products Infrastructure stack, shifting mindset and treating data as a product, enabling users to share, discover and utilize data from other domains — maximizing and delivering data-driven value at scale. Using cutting-edge technologies, Reply implemented an innovative data-mesh solution, leading to better decision making, saving costs and boosting revenues. Reply tracks compliance with project specifications and milestones to achieve the customer's data mesh goal.



## Business Needs

#### 1. Scalable Data Management Without Governance Compromise

Existing data management structures caused bottlenecks and silos, requiring a scalable solution that maintains strict data governance standards.

#### 2. Data-Driven Value at Scale

The customer needed to shift its organizational mindset to treat data as a product, enabling teams to share, discover, and utilize data across domains to maximize data-driven value.

#### 3. Robust and Future-Proof Data Infrastructure

A strong infrastructure foundation was needed to support long-term data product management, federated governance, and self-service capabilities across the organization.



## Provided Services
### Cloud/ onPrem: Hybrid Cloud / On-Premise

#### 1. Agile Project Planning & Steering

Reply supports the planning and steering of the project and development team through Agile principles, dedicated roles, and ceremonies — ensuring effective collaboration, prompt issue resolution, and continuous improvement.

#### 2. Agile IT-PEP Adherence & Stakeholder Communication

Reply experts facilitate adherence to agile IT-PEP procedures, documentation, and processes, promoting transparent communication among stakeholders and timely milestone achievement according to the project timeline.

#### 3. Data Lake Analytics Platform Implementation

The data lake analytics platform enables seamless connectivity to diverse data sources, eliminating the need for data movement. It ensures fast and accurate access to on-premise, cloud, or hybrid cloud data supporting federated queries with fine-grained access controls to protect sensitive data.

#### 4. Data-Mesh Solution Design & Implementation

Based on interactive workshops, all aspects and expectations of the existing infrastructure were evaluated and a robust data-mesh solution was implemented, making the customer a data-driven company.

#### 5. Federated Governance & Self-Service Data Platform

Emphasizing data as a valuable product, the implementation includes federated computational governance and a self-service data platform, promoting collaborative engagement within the new data management system.

## Mastering Challenges

#### 1. Authorization and Monitoring Infrastructure Integration

Integrating the data-mesh solution with the customer's existing authorization and monitoring infrastructure posed significant technical complexity, requiring deep alignment with internal security and compliance standards.

#### 2. Tailored Workflow Implementation for Unique Operational Needs

Implementing customer-requested workflows demanded a highly tailored solution to address unique operational requirements, going beyond standard approaches to fit the specific processes and constraints of the organization.

#### 3. Cultural Mindset Shift Towards Data as a Product

Driving the organizational change needed to treat data as a product required overcoming deeply rooted cultural habits and resistance, necessitating continuous coaching, communication, and stakeholder engagement across domains.
