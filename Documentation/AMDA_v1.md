## Project Insights
> Ongoing DevOps project for custom application monitoring of workloads on a Big Data platform based on OpenShift. The objective was to improve development and deployment processes for monitoring, alerting, and visualization of metrics and logs, increasing the monitoring team's efficiency. The solution was rolled out to three production applications of the Data Analytics Platform; further rollouts are ongoing or planned.



## Business Needs

#### 1. Improve transparency across systems

Create a shared understanding of platform stability, bottlenecks, and early anomalies by making monitoring and observability information centralized, consistent, and understandable. This enables operations, development, and business stakeholders to identify whether issues are infrastructure-, application-, or process-related before incidents become acute.

#### 2. Accelerate incident root-cause analysis

Improve observability so that logs, metrics, and events can be correlated and structured consistently. This supports faster identification of affected components and dependencies, reduces downtime and response times, and improves coordination between teams.

#### 3. Enable continuous operational improvement

Provide a consistent, usable view of stability, availability, performance, error frequency, and recurring anomalies for technical teams and project stakeholders. This supports early risk identification, better prioritization of measures, and long-term operational stability.



## Provided Services
### Cloud/ onPrem: OnPrem

#### 1. Automated deployment pipeline

Implemented a CI/CD pipeline for automated deployment of dashboards, alerts, and workloads.

#### 2. Infrastructure-as-Code workspace

Provided an Infrastructure-as-Code workspace with Helm packages and Docker images.

#### 3. Custom monitoring and visualization

Developed a custom monitoring tool for Trino queries and Grafana dashboards for metric visualization, providing real-time visibility of system health and resource consumption.
