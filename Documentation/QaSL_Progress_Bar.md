## Project Insights
> With the need for a strong foundation for future data management, the purpose of the project is to reduce bottlenecks and silos in data management while allowing scalability – without compromising on data governance. The goal is to have an efficient Data Products Infrastructure stack, shifting mindset and treating data as a product, enabling users to share, discover and utilize data from other domains – maximizing and delivering data-driven value at scale. Using cutting-edge technologies, Reply implemented an innovative data-mesh solution, leading to better decision making, saving costs and boosting revenues for VW. Reply tracks compliance with project specifications and milestones to achieve VW data mesh goal.

## Business Needs

#### 1. Elimination of Data Bottlenecks and Silos

The customer required a solution to break down existing bottlenecks and silos in data management, enabling seamless data flow across organizational domains.

#### 2. Scalable Data Products Infrastructure

There was a need for a scalable Data Products Infrastructure stack that allows different domains to share, discover, and utilize data efficiently without sacrificing governance.

#### 3. Data-Driven Decision Making at Scale

The customer needed to shift towards a data-driven culture, maximizing data-driven value at scale through better decision making and cost savings.

## Provided Services
### Cloud/ onPrem: On-Premise

#### 1. Agile Project Planning and Steering

Reply supports the planning and steering of the project and development team through Agile principles, dedicated roles, and ceremonies – ensuring effective collaboration, prompt issue resolution, and continuous improvement.

#### 2. Agile IT-PEP Adherence and Stakeholder Communication

Reply experts facilitate adherence to agile IT-PEP procedures, documentation, and processes, promoting transparent communication among stakeholders and timely milestone achievement according to the project timeline.

#### 3. Data Lake Analytics Platform Implementation

The data lake analytics platform enables seamless connectivity to diverse data sources, eliminating the need for data movement. It ensures fast and accurate access to on-premise, cloud, or hybrid cloud data, supporting federated queries using fine-grained access controls to protect sensitive data.

#### 4. Data-Mesh Solution Design and Implementation

Based on interactive workshops, all aspects and expectations of the existing infrastructure were evaluated and a robust data-mesh solution was implemented by Reply, which made VW a data-driven company. The implementation includes federated computational governance and a self-service data platform, promoting collaborative engagement within the new data management system.

## Mastering Challenges

#### 1. VW Authorization and Monitoring Infrastructure Integration

Integrating the data-mesh solution with VW's existing authorization and monitoring infrastructure posed significant technical complexity, requiring deep alignment with internal security and compliance standards.

#### 2. Tailored Workflow Implementation for Unique Operational Needs

Implementing VW-requested workflows demanded a highly tailored solution to address unique operational requirements, going beyond standard approaches to fit the specific processes and constraints of the organization.

#### 3. Cultural Mindset Shift Towards Data as a Product

Driving the organizational change needed to treat data as a product required overcoming deeply rooted cultural habits and resistance, necessitating continuous coaching, communication, and stakeholder engagement across domains.
